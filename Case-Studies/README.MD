# Case Studies

The **Case-Studies** folder contains all documented structural events observed across Division‑K markets.  
Each case study captures a real engine cycle, spike sequence, actor interaction, timing window, or structural anomaly.

Case studies are the practical, real‑world evidence of the Master Schema, Proto‑Database, Image Library, and Structural Theory.

---

## Purpose of Case Studies

Case studies exist to:

- document real structural engine behavior

- provide visual and written evidence of spikes, actors, timing, and movement families

- support pattern ontology development

- validate SQL tables with real market examples

- create a historical archive of engine cycles

- train future dashboards and machine‑assisted tools

They are the “field research” component of Division‑K.

---

## What a Case Study Contains

Each case study includes:

- market identity

- event summary

- structural context

- spike or actor sequence

- engine behavior analysis

- timing window or ignition pattern

- diagrams from the Image Library

- schema layer references

- structural implications

Case studies follow a consistent Markdown format for readability and research continuity.
