# Master Schema (20‑Layer Structural Market Model)

The **Master Schema** is the full 20‑layer structural framework used to map synthetic and natural market‑engine behavior.  
Each layer represents a specific component of market structure, engine motion, actor influence, spike behavior, timing sync, candle structure, liquidation modeling, or pattern ontology.

This folder is the **brain** of the entire Division‑K research system.

---

## Purpose of This Module

The Master Schema exists to:

- break the market into repeatable structural parts

- track engine cycles, movement families, and actor behavior

- define spike ignition logic and timing synchronization

- classify candle behavior across multiple timeframes

- map liquidation cascades and recovery signatures

- organize structural research into a consistent, scalable framework

- connect directly to the Proto‑Database and Image Library

It is the conceptual and organizational foundation of Division‑K.
