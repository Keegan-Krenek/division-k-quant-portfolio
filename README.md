# Division K Quant Portfolio
Market Microstructure • Synthetic‑Engine Research • Structural Trading Systems • SQL Proto‑Database

A fully structured, multi‑layer quantitative research portfolio built to reverse‑engineer synthetic market engines, extract repeatable structural signals, and support production‑grade trading research. This repository demonstrates end‑to‑end market microstructure analysis, schema architecture, database design, and real trading execution.

##  Portfolio Highlights
- 20‑Layer Structural Trading Schema mapping full synthetic‑engine behavior  
- SQL Proto‑Database with dependency‑ordered schema, ERD, and engine‑level tables  
- Synthetic‑Engine Research including actor classification, spike ignition mapping, drift floors, burst corridors  
- 32,000+ executed trades across multiple market conditions  
- High‑frequency microstructure execution (250 APM)  
- Case studies with annotated engine cycles and structural outcomes  
- Production‑ready research architecture designed for scalability and repeatability
